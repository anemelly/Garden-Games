House rules

  • Exactly five in a row wins: across, down, or diagonal.
  • Six or more in a row does not count.
  • No double threes: you can’t place a piece that makes two open threes at once (threes with both ends free, including broken ones like X X _ X). Double fours and a four plus a three are fine, and a move that makes five always wins. This applies to everyone, Claude included.
  • Game 1 starts with a dice roll. Higher number goes first; a tie rolls again.
  • After each game, the loser starts the next one. A draw goes back to the dice.
  • You play flowers, Claude plays monstera leaves. You can change your flower between games.
  • The newest piece wiggles until the other player moves, so you always see the last move.
  • Claude looks several moves ahead and hunts for chains of fours.
  • Watch mode: Claude Ginkgo (golden leaves) plays Claude Monstera, both on hard. Same rules: dice for game 1, then the loser starts. Their wins go in a separate tally.
  • Play online: host a room and send the link, or join with a code. Same rules: dice for game 1, then the loser starts. If you both picked the same flower, the guest plays ginkgo leaves. Online games get their own room score.
  • Columns are A–O and rows 1–15, so move notes can name each spot.

How it works

  • 2 to 8 players on one device. Play with just people, or add Claude bots to fill seats. At least one player needs to be a person.
  • Spin the flower wheel (1–10) and crawl forward. Pass a payday and you still get paid.
  • Big moments stop you: the start fork, graduation, the wedding arbor, the home market, the two forks and retirement.
  • Safe paths are steady. Risky paths are shorter, with bigger wins and losses, casinos and business deals.
  • Trade garden stocks before you spin. Prices move every round, with the odd boom or crash.
  • At retirement: 1 Bloom per $20k (debt costs extra), 10 for a spouse, 6 per kid, 3 per pet, your home, 5 for a degree, 2 per promotion, plus life Blooms and retirement bonuses.
